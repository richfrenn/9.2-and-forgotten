# 9.2 and Forgotten: Analysis plan, Amendment 4 (recognition census)

Written 12 November 2026, before any album outside the two 50-album samples was scored against the recognition lists.

## Why

Two reviews of the first draft (12 November) argued that (a) the Pitchfork-versus-comparison recognition gap rests mainly on the 34 NME-only comparison albums, which are largely British, while five of the six recognition lists are American; and (b) the comparison that best isolates Pitchfork from other American critics, against the 14 Pazz & Jop albums, is underpowered (1 of 14, p = 0.09). Recognition requires no listening data, so it can be scored for every eligible album rather than for samples.

## What had been seen when this was written

Everything listed in Amendment 3, plus: full-list recognition for the 100 sampled albums (17 of 50 Pitchfork, 1 of 50 comparison; 0 of 34 NME-only, 1 of 14 Pazz & Jop-only, 0 of 2 both); the within-sample models; the full contents of the six lists in the course of scoring the samples. No album outside the samples has been looked up on any list.

## Census

- Pitchfork: all 276 eligible albums from the 2006-2011 year-end top 50s (same exclusions as the sample frame).
- Comparison: all 318 eligible albums in the comparison pool (NME and Pazz & Jop year-end top 50s 2006-2011, not on any Pitchfork top 50), classified by source: NME only (184), Pazz & Jop only (103), both (31).
- Outcome: appearance anywhere on any of the six main lists (primary, as in Amendment 3); top-100 appearance (secondary). Decade lists are not used in the census.
- Matching: by artist and title against each list's entries for albums released 2005 to 2012; every candidate match and near-match checked by hand for identity before any test is run. Matching is done for all 594 albums at once, blind to group where practical.

## Tests

1. Primary: Pitchfork against Pazz & Jop-only albums (the American comparison), Fisher's exact test and the difference in proportions with a 95% interval.
2. Pitchfork against NME-only and against "both", the same way; pooled comparison reported but secondary.
3. Within Pitchfork (n = 276): Firth logistic regression of any appearance on log year-end rank; release year added as a check. Design weights are not needed (census).
4. Sensitivity: Guardian list alone (the British list); American lists alone.

The 50-album sample results are kept and reported; the census results supersede them for recognition. The listening analysis is unchanged.
